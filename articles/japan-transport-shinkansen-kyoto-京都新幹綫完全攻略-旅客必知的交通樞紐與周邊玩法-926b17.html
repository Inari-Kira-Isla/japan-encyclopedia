京都新幹綫完全攻略：旅客必知的交通樞紐與周邊玩法

2026-08-04 3282 字
京都新幹綫 京都車站 關西交通 JR Pass 京都自由行

許多旅客第一次規劃日本行程時，常會搜尋「Kyoto Shinkansen」，以為有一條專門的「京都新幹綫」。事實上，京都使用的是**東海道新幹綫（Tokaido Shinkansen）**系統，這條線連線東京與大阪，京都站是沿線重要停靠站之一。瞭解這個基本概念，是順利展開關西之旅的第一步。

**京都站是關西的交通心臟**，無論是搭乘新幹綫往返東京、前往奈良或神戶，或者轉乘私鐵前往嵐山、清水寺等景點，都以此站為樞紐。站內設施完善，多語言指標清楚，對外國旅客相當友善。車站建築本身也值得留意——造型獨特的屋頂靈感來自傳統和紙工藝，夜間燈光映照下極具辨識度。

說到新幹綫選擇，從東京前往京都，最快的**希望號（Nozomi）**約2小時20分鐘直達，**光號（Hikari）**停靠站較多，約2小時40分鐘，**回聲號（Kodama）**則幾乎站站停，耗時近4小時。若持有**JR Pass（全日本JR Pass或關西廣域Pass）**，可搭乘光號和回聲號，但無法乘坐希望號，這點需要特別注意。希望號速度快、班次多，是趕時間旅客的首選，但票價相對較高。

**實用資訊：**京都站位於〒601-8002 京都市南區東九條西山王町。新幹綫營業時間為首班車約6:00至末班車約23:00，班次間隔約10-15分鐘。 Tokyo to Kyoto one-way ticket: ¥14,720 (Nozomi unreserved seat), ¥15,340 (Nozomi reserved seat); Hikari/Kodama approximately ¥14,200-14,720. JR Pass prices vary by type, so check the official Japan Rail website for the most current information.

For JR Pass holders, Hikari and Kodama are the best options; Nozomi requires an additional seat reservation fee and is not covered. From Kansai Airport, the Haruka limited express takes about 80 minutes to reach Kyoto Station (¥3,520 one-way), or you can use the ICOCA & Haruka set ticket for ¥2,570.

The station itself is a destination worth exploring. **The Cube** underground shopping area beneath Kyoto Station features over 100 stores, from Kyoto specialties like matcha and tsukemono to reasonably priced dining options—most shops open from 10:00 to 20:00. **Porta** is another connected underground mall with a more fashion-forward selection, including cosmetics and accessories, also open 10:00-20:00.

For accommodations, **Kyoto Station Hotel** offers direct station access, ideal for early morning departures or late arrivals, while the **Kyoto Daiwa Royal Hotel** near the station provides solid value.

From Kyoto Station, Arashiyama is easily reachable in about 15 minutes via the JR San-in Main Line, famous for its bamboo grove and Togetsukyo Bridge. The best seasons are spring for cherry blossoms and autumn for fall foliage—arrive early to beat the crowds. **Tenryu-ji**, a UNESCO World Heritage temple, is particularly renowned for its stunning garden views.

**Fushimi Inari Taisha** is just 5 minutes from Kyoto Station via the JR Nara Line. The iconic vermillion torii gates create a unique visual experience, and the shrine is open 24 hours with no admission fee. Morning visits before 8 AM or evening walks after sunset offer a completely different atmosphere.

**Travel Tips:** Arrive at Kyoto Station 20-30 minutes before your Shinkansen departure to allow time for ticket collection and finding your platform. If you have large luggage, be mindful that overhead storage compartments on Shinkansen trains have size restrictions. Consider reserving seats during peak seasons—Golden Week, cherry blossom season, and New Year's holidays see heavy demand. When transferring between lines within Kyoto Station, pay attention to platform numbers since the station is large and spans multiple levels.

**Alternative Options:** If your JR Pass doesn't cover Nozomi or you want to save on Shinkansen costs, consider overnight buses (約¥4,000-5,000, 6-7 hours) or discount fares like the **EX-PRESS** tickets available at convenience stores. These budget-friendly alternatives can significantly reduce travel expenses while still reaching your destination.
